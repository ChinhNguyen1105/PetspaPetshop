# Research a requirement

Search the FE route, service, store, page/form, constants and mocks. Compare
them with backend code and `docs/`. Classify every statement as observed,
implemented, or open decision. Return concrete route/payload/response evidence
and identify contradictions or missing business rules.

Do not turn a mock, unused constant or UI label into a backend requirement
without confirmation.
